\## Customer Portal



Allows customers to:



\- Purchase policies

\- View policies

\- Submit claims

\- Track claim progress





\## Claims Management System



Used by the Claims Department.



Functions:



\- Claim Registration

\- Investigation

\- Approval

\- Payment Tracking





\## Underwriting System



Used to:



\- Assess risk

\- Calculate premiums

\- Approve insurance policies





\## Finance System



Used for:



\- Payments

\- Accounting

\- Payroll

\- Reporting





\## Human Resource Management System



Used to manage:



\- Employees

\- Leave

\- Payroll

\- Performance





\## Email System



Primary internal communication platform.





\## Active Directory



Provides:



\- Authentication

\- Authorization

\- Identity Management
