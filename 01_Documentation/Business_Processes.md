\## Purpose



This document describes the core business processes of Phoenix Assurance LTD.







\## 1. Customer Registration



A prospective customer visits a branch office, the company website, or the mobile application.



Customer information is collected and verified before creating a customer account.





\## 2. Insurance Quotation



The customer requests an insurance quotation.



The system calculates the premium based on:



\- Customer profile

\- Insurance product

\- Risk assessment

\- Coverage amount





\## 3. Policy Purchase



The customer accepts the quotation.



Payment is processed.



A policy number is generated.



The customer receives policy documents.





\## 4. Premium Payment



Customers can pay through:



\- Bank Transfer

\- Mobile Money

\- Debit/Credit Card



Payments are recorded in the Finance System.





\## 5. Claims Management



Customer submits a claim.



Claims Department reviews documentation.



Fraud checks are performed.



Claim is approved or rejected.



Approved claims are paid through Finance.





\## 6. Policy Renewal



Customers receive renewal reminders.



Policies may be renewed automatically or manually.





\## 7. Customer Support



Customers may:



\- Ask questions

\- Report issues

\- Update personal information

\- Track claims



Support is available through:



\- Branch offices

\- Phone

\- Email

\- Online Portal
