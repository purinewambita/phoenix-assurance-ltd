Phoenix Assurance LTD shall implement security controls to protect business operations.



\## Confidentiality



Only authorized users may access sensitive information.





\## Integrity



Business information must not be modified without authorization.





\## Availability



Critical systems shall remain available with minimal downtime.





\## Authentication



All users must authenticate before accessing company systems.





\## Authorization



Access shall follow the Principle of Least Privilege.





\## Logging



All security events shall be logged and monitored.





\## Backup



Critical systems shall be backed up regularly.





\## Encryption



Sensitive information shall be encrypted during storage and transmission.





\## Compliance



The company shall comply with applicable laws, regulations, and internal policies.
